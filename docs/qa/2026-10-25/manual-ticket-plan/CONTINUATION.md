## Confirmed trading UI defects and exact fixes

| Defect | Reproduction | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Expanded Buy loses signal provenance | Row Buy supplies fromSignal and planStop; expanded Quote Buy and calculator Buy did not. The ticket skipped the client plan warning. Server admission still applied. | BoardTicket forwards canonical stop and signal origin to Quote Buy; calculator receives a separate signalPlanStop, independent of editable preview levels, and forwards it to the ticket. |
| Same contract retains old plan fields | Reopen an existing ticket for the same symbol/exchange/side with entry 150, qty 100 and stop 120 after entry 100/qty 50/stop 80; old ticket retained price 100/qty 50 and blocked against the new stop. | Ticket identity includes incoming price, quantity, lot size, product, presets, source stop and tag. Reopening loads the new ticket and its bracket presets together. |
| New instrument discards bracket presets | Switch a cash ticket with -5%/10% presets to another with -2%/20%; old reset cleared both. | Restore the incoming presets after instrument reset, including the appropriate ticket tab. |
| Late margin reply belongs to previous ticket | Old request resolves after another instrument is loaded; old required amount populated the new ticket. | Invalidate the previous request generation immediately and clear old margin/charge state on handoff. |
| Signal stop follows unrelated search selection | Search inside a signal ticket and choose a different instrument; the old signal stop could disable its Buy. | Apply signal provenance and stop warnings only while symbol and exchange match the source signal. |
